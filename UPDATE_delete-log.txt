UPDATE — "Delete log" option in the HR EMAIL column
===================================================

WHAT THIS ADDS
In the Candidate pipeline, the HR EMAIL column now has a "Delete log" button
next to the send status:
  - When a row shows "Sent"   -> a "Delete log" button appears under it.
  - When a row shows "Failed" -> "Resend" and "Delete log" both appear.
Clicking "Delete log" clears that candidate's HR-email send status, so the
column goes back to "Not sent" (with its Send button). It asks for confirmation
first.

IMPORTANT — this only clears the EMAIL LOG (the Sent/Failed marker). It does NOT
delete the candidate, the application, the resume, or anything else. Everything
else stays exactly as it is.
