UPDATE — "Delete log" removes the whole candidate row
=====================================================

WHAT THIS DOES
The "Delete log" button in the HR EMAIL column now DELETES THE WHOLE ROW
(the candidate / application), not just the email status.

  - Click "Delete log" on any row -> a confirmation pops up naming the
    candidate (e.g. "Delete suba (BSC-2611-0232) permanently?").
  - Confirm -> the application and its status history are permanently removed
    and the row disappears from the pipeline.

Safeguards built in:
  - It asks for confirmation first, showing the candidate's name + reference,
    so a row can't be deleted by a stray click.
  - If the candidate had already been made an employee (status Joined), the
    EMPLOYEE record is KEPT (just unlinked) — you don't lose a staff record.
  - Resume / offer-letter files already in OneDrive are left in place.

** This is permanent — deleted applications cannot be recovered from the app. **
